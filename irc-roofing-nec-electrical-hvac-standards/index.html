1. IRC Chapter 9 Roofing & Emergency Storm Tarping

Under IRC Section R905.2, asphalt shingle installations require approved self-adhering polymer-modified bitumen ice barriers extending at least 24 inches inside the exterior wall line in cold climates, corrosion-resistant drip edge flashing (R905.2.8.5), and six-nail high-wind fastening in hurricane/derecho zones.
