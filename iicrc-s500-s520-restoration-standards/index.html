ANSI/IICRC S500 Water Damage Mitigation & S520 Mold Remediation Standards (2026)

Structural water mitigation and microbial remediation across the United States follow the consensus engineering standards published by the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification: ANSI/IICRC S500 (Water Damage Restoration) and ANSI/IICRC S520 (Mold Remediation). Verified directory dispatch and RSMeans cost benchmarks are published at 1800HomeUSA Water Damage Mitigation and 1800HomeUSA Mold Remediation.

1. ANSI/IICRC S500 Water Contamination Categories & Evaporation Classes

  • Category 1 (Clean Water): Broken potable supply lines, tub overflows, or appliance supply hoses. Can degrade to Category 2 within 24–48 hours if left stagnant on porous building materials.
  • Category 2 (Gray Water): Washing machine discharges, dishwasher overflows, or hydro-static seepage containing chemical or biological contaminants. Requires antimicrobial surface treatment and pad removal.
  • Category 3 (Black Water / Pathogenic): Sanitary sewer backups, toilet overflows with fecal matter, or overland riverine/stormwater flooding. Requires full removal of affected porous drywall (typically 12 to 24 inches above the high-water mark), carpet, insulation, and subfloor sanitization.
